What I need from you

  1. Home screen: A, B, C, or a mix.
  2. Nudges for the pilot: my suggestion is Subscription or bitcoin, Payday, Price went up, New subscription, Paying twice and Weekly summary. Add, drop, or reword.
  3. Where nudges reach people: in the app only, or also by email? A web app can’t reliably send push notifications to iPhones unless the customer adds it to their home screen.
  4. How many a week: a cap, so FiCo doesn’t nag. I’d start at three.
