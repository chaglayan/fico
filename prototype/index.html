Rules this prototype assumes
  • FiCo fills in, Xcoins confirms. Every buy and every new autobuy is confirmed in the Xcoins window with a one-time code.
  • Presets are signed with FiCo's partner key and expire after 10 minutes. Xcoins rejects presets that are unsigned, expired or already used.
  • A wallet address from FiCo counts as new until the customer confirms it inside Xcoins.
  • FiCo can cancel an autobuy through the API. Starting or raising one needs the Xcoins window.
  • Xcoins can hold buys and ask for documents at any time. FiCo passes the request on and sends the customer to Xcoins to answer.
  • FiCo doesn't suggest what or when to buy. It carries out what the customer asks for.
