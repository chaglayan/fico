What stays the same in every direction

  • FiCo is its own brand. Xcoins only appears where crypto is involved, as "Crypto with Xcoins".
  • Onboarding starts with FiCo: welcome, a few questions, connect a bank, then the first home screen. Crypto is offered later, and Xcoins verifies the customer only when they choose crypto.
  • Every buy still ends in the Xcoins window with a one-time code. It will be restyled to sit comfortably next to whichever direction you pick.
